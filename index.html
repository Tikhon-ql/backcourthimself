What the app does

backcourthimself is a desktop application that the creator uses to manage the TikTok accounts of the backcourthimself basketball channel. With TikTok's permission it lets the account owner:

  • sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) in the system browser;
  • see the account's basic profile and public statistics (followers, likes, video count);
  • see the list of the account's public videos with their views, likes, comments and shares;
  • upload a finished video from the computer to the TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner reviews and publishes inside the TikTok app.

The app runs only on the user's own computer. It has no server and no user accounts of its own.
