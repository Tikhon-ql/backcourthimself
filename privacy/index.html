Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what data the backcourthimself desktop application ("the App") accesses through TikTok and how it is handled.

Data we access

When you sign in with TikTok and grant permissions, the App may receive:

  • user.info.basic: your open ID, display name and avatar;
  • user.info.stats: follower, following, like and video counts;
  • video.list: your public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts;
  • video.upload: permission to send a video you select to your TikTok inbox as a draft.

The App only requests the permissions you enable, and you can decline any of them on the TikTok consent screen.

How data is stored

All data stays on your own computer. Access tokens, refresh tokens, settings and cached statistics are stored in a local file encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from a password only you know. The App has no server, so your data is never sent to us or to anyone other than TikTok.

How data is used

Data is used only to show you your own account information and to upload videos you choose. We do not sell, share, rent or use your data for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Third parties

The App communicates only with TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com). TikTok's handling of your data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept on your device until you remove the account in the App or delete the local data file. You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
